Troubleshooting

Things go wrong for everyone. Find your symptom, read the cause, try the fix. Use your browser's search (Ctrl+F) to look for the words in your error message.

Five checks that solve half of all problems
  1. Does the prompt show (lerobot)? If not: conda activate lerobot.
  2. Is the power supply plugged in at both ends, and the USB cable? USB does not power the motors.
  3. Has the port name changed? Run lerobot-find-port again.
  4. Are you using exactly the same --robot.id, --teleop.id and --robot.cameras text as before?
  5. Unplug the arm's power for five seconds and plug it back in. This clears most motor error states.

Fixes are marked by where they come from: Official = LeRobot documentation or maintainers; Vendor = the kit maker's guide; Community = reported by other builders and not confirmed officially.

Still stuck?

  1. Copy the last few lines of the error message. The final line usually names the real problem.
  2. Search for it in the LeRobot issues on GitHub. Someone has very often met it before.
  3. Ask on the LeRobot Discord. Include your operating system, the output of lerobot-info, the exact command and the full error. Never include your access token.
