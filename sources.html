How this course was researched

Every command and number was taken from the sources below as they stood on 16–17 November 2026, when the current LeRobot release was 0.7.0. LeRobot changes quickly, so if a command here stops working, the official page for that step is the first place to look.

What we could not verify

Honesty about the limits of this course:

  • Nothing here was tested on a physical SO-101. The commands are copied from official documentation; the Windows versions are our adaptation of them (one line, COM ports), because the official docs only show macOS and Linux.
  • The Windows NVIDIA install step (chapter 4, step 4) is based on what PyTorch's download server offers, not on an official LeRobot instruction.
  • Assembly times and typical success rates come from individual builders' reports, not from official figures.
  • Training time on free Google Colab is unknown; official estimates assume faster paid GPUs.
  • The exact appearance of program output (the grey "what you should see" boxes) is reconstructed from documentation and source code, and may differ in detail.
  • The interactive demos are simplified simulations built to show one idea each. Their numbers are illustrative, not measurements of a real arm or a real policy.
